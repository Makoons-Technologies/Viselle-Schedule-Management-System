# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 13, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Makoons Technologies** (“Makoons,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use **Viselle**, our scheduling and online booking software for salons, spas, and other beauty businesses (the “Service”). That includes viselle.net, www.viselle.net, hosted booking pages such as `yourspa.viselle.net`, and related apps and APIs.

Viselle is the platform. The salon, spa, or studio you book with (the “Business”) is the business providing your appointment.

If you have questions, contact us at [hello@viselle.net](mailto:hello@viselle.net). Company site: [https://makoonstech.com/](https://makoonstech.com/).

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Clients** who book or manage appointments online, or whose information is entered by a Business.
- **Business users** (owners and staff) who create accounts to run their schedule on Viselle.
- **Website visitors** to viselle.net marketing pages.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you or a Business provide

- **Booking details:** first name, last name, email address, optional phone number, appointment notes, service, provider, date and time.
- **Account details:** name, email, password (stored as a hash), business name, address, city, phone, staff and service information, and similar settings.
- **Support:** messages you send to hello@viselle.net or through in-app support tickets, including attachments you choose to upload.
- **Payments (Businesses):** subscription and billing information processed by **Stripe**. We do not store full card numbers on Viselle servers.

Phone number is **optional**. We use it for appointment texts only if you **opt in** (see SMS below). Leaving the phone field blank means we will not text you.

### Information collected automatically

- Device and browser information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, pages viewed, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and secure the Service.
- Cookies and similar storage used for sign-in, security, and remembering preferences. See **Cookies** below.

### SMS and messaging

If you provide a phone number and **opt in** to texts, we (and the Business you booked with) may use that number to send appointment reminders, booking confirmations, reschedule or cancellation notices, and related transactional messages — **only when SMS sending is actually enabled** for that environment.

**Honesty about delivery today:** transactional **email** reminders are the live production reminder path. Production SMS / A2P / toll-free registration is **not fully complete**. Staging may send texts for testing. Marketing or this policy should not be read as a guarantee that appointment texts are live on viselle.net for every shop. When SMS is sent, we use **ClickSend** (a Sinch company) as the delivery processor.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply **HELP** for help and **STOP** to opt out. We do not sell your phone number.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, and improve Viselle (scheduling, booking pages, reminders, account administration).
- Send transactional **email** about appointments and accounts.
- Send SMS about appointments **only if you have opted in and SMS sending is enabled**.
- Authenticate users, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the Service secure.
- Communicate with Businesses about their accounts, billing, and product updates.
- Comply with law and enforce our [Terms](/terms).

We do **not** use client booking phone numbers for third-party marketing or advertising lists.

## 4. How we share information

We share information with:

- **The Business you booked with.** Your booking name, contact details, appointment, and notes are available to that Business and its staff so they can serve you.
- **Service providers** that help us run Viselle:
  - **Stripe** — Business subscription and payment processing.
  - **ClickSend** — SMS delivery when texts are actually sent.
  - **Hosting and infrastructure** providers that serve viselle.net and store account and booking data.
  - Email delivery providers for transactional messages.
- **Legal and safety.** If required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. SMS consent and opt-out

SMS for appointments is **opt-in**. The checkbox on a booking form is **not** pre-checked. Consent is stored for that Business and is not treated as consent for unrelated marketing from Viselle.

To stop texts: reply **STOP** to any appointment message, or ask the Business to remove your phone number. After opt-out we will not send further appointment SMS to that number, except a single confirmation of the opt-out if required by carriers. Reply **HELP** or email [hello@viselle.net](mailto:hello@viselle.net) for help.

Leaving the phone field blank, or leaving the checkbox unchecked, means we will not send you SMS.

## 6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar local storage to keep you signed in, protect accounts, and remember display preferences (for example theme or sidebar state). These are needed for the Service to work.

We do **not** currently load advertising, retargeting, or analytics cookies on marketing pages. A separate Cookie Policy page is not published yet. If we add non-essential tracking later, we will update this section and add consent where required.

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts so pages can display consistently. That is a third-party request to serve typefaces, not an advertising tracker we control.

You can control cookies in your browser; some features (such as staying signed in) will not work without them.

## 7. Retention

We keep booking and account data for as long as the Business account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Businesses may update or delete client records they control. You may ask us or the Business to correct or delete your information as described below.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request:

- Clients: contact the Business you booked with, and/or email [hello@viselle.net](mailto:hello@viselle.net).
- Business users: use account settings where available, or email us.

We may need to verify your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 9. Children

Viselle is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 11. International processing

We are based in the United States. If you access Viselle from another country, your information may be processed in the United States and other locations where we or our processors operate.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change, and the current version will be posted at [/privacy](/privacy). Material changes may also be noted in product or email notices to Business users.
